SpaceX

What is SpaceX?

SpaceX is a American aerospace manufacturer, designer, and manufacturer of spacecraft and launch vehicles.

Or in other words, SpaceX is a company with great ambition.

What is SpaceX doing?

  • SpaceX is developing the Starship spacecraft, which is designed to carry humans to Mars and other destinations in the solar system.
  • SpaceX is also working on the Falcon 9 rocket, which is used to launch starlink satellites and cargo into space.
  • SpaceX is also working on the starmind project, which is a AI training project that trains AI models in orbit.
